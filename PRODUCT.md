# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Young women, roughly 18-24 (Gen Z), shopping for everyday clothing. They want relaxed, comfortable cotton pieces they can move in, browsing on mobile-first, trend-aware, and expecting the shopping experience itself to feel current and engaging, not just the product photography.

## Product Purpose

Zella sells relaxed-fit cotton shirts and trousers for girls/young women — soft fabric, room to move, made for everyday wear. The site's job is to sell the current season's cotton edit (shirts + trousers) and build brand affinity.

## Positioning

Comfort-first, breathable cotton with a relaxed fit — "loose cotton, made to move." Distinguishes on fabric feel and fit philosophy (roomy where it counts) rather than being a fast-fashion trend-chaser or a technical/performance brand.

## Operating Context

Single-page storefront (hero/landing) with primary nav sections: Shirts, Trousers, Our Story, and a cart. Shoppers browse the current colorway/cotton edit and move into shop/lookbook flows from here.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js app (App Router), Tailwind CSS v4 tokens, Motion (`motion/react`) for animation, custom cursor interaction on fine-pointer devices.
- Confirmed sections/copy to preserve as product fact: brand name "Zella," tagline "Loose Cotton, Made to Move," hero copy ("Relaxed shirts and trousers cut from breathable cotton — roomy where it counts, soft against the skin, made for girls who don't sit still."), nav items (Shirts, Trousers, Our Story), cart, "Shop the edit" / "See the lookbook" CTAs, ticker items (100% cotton, relaxed fit, shirts & trousers, for girls who move, new season).
- Existing colorway names (Sky, Butter, Lilac, Mocha, Burgundy) are current-season product facts, not fixed brand constraints — a redesign may introduce a new colorway system for the new visual world, but should not silently drop the "colorways" concept itself.
- Product photography on hand: `/public/shirt-blue-stripe.jpg`, `/public/shirt-burgundy.jpg`, `/public/shirt-lilac.jpg`, `/public/shirt-mocha-stripe.jpg`, `/public/shirt-sky-stripe.jpg`, `/public/shirt-yellow-stripe.jpg`. No other real photography exists — do not fabricate additional product shots as if real.
- Real brand: do not invent testimonials, pricing, sizing details, press mentions, or founder/story content that isn't already present. Flag where real content would be needed instead of inventing it as fact.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Zella.
- Current tagline: "Loose Cotton, Made to Move" (open to redesign as part of the visual-world change, since this request is a rebrand of look/feel, not just a refinement).

## Evidence on Hand

- Six real product photos in `/public` (see Capabilities and Constraints).
- No customer testimonials, press, pricing, or sizing data exist yet.

## Product Principles

1. Comfort and movement are the product truth — the redesign can change how that's expressed visually, not what's promised.
2. Speak to an 18-24 Gen Z shopper: current, trend-literate, mobile-first, expects the site itself to feel alive.
3. Real product photography exists and should stay the hero evidence — style the site around it rather than replacing it with invented content.
4. This is a real brand — don't fabricate facts (pricing, testimonials, press) the business hasn't provided.
